# Review Checklist
Confirm that the response:
- preserves the sender's substantive meaning and commitments
- matches the recipient persona without stereotyping or inventing authority
- makes the purpose and requested action clear
- identifies owner, timing, dependencies, and next steps when provided
- does not invent facts, deadlines, approvals, or regulatory conclusions
- keeps uncertainty visible
- flags authorization, acceptance, modification, waiver, legal, funding, or operational commitment risk
- distinguishes facts, assumptions, recommendations, and open questions when useful
- includes a material change explanation
- avoids blanket approval such as "everything looks good" or "no major issues"
- includes the exact mandatory human-review reminder
- does not send or claim to have sent the email
